Claimwell Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Claimwell is published by Vaulith LLC ("we"). This policy covers the Claimwell app for iPhone and Android and the Claimwell website.

The short version

We don't collect your personal information. Claimwell has no accounts and no servers that receive your data. What you enter stays on your phone (or, on the website, in your browser), and you can erase it at any time.

What you enter, and where it stays

To match you with settlements and get you ready to file, Claimwell asks for:

  • Your answers to the eligibility questions (for example, whether you bought a product, used a service, or were a patient at a hospital).
  • Your details, if you choose to add them: name, mailing address, email address and phone number.
  • Claim details, if you choose to add them: things a claim form asks for, such as a notice ID, a serial number or a vehicle identification number.
  • Your claims: the settlements you mark as filed, confirmation numbers, how you chose to be paid, and payments you record.

All of this is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We can't see it, and we can't recover it if you lose your device.

Some answers can say something sensitive about you: for example, that you were a patient at a particular hospital or used a health service. They stay on your device like everything else.

When you file a claim

Claimwell opens each settlement's official claim website. When you tap Fill my details, the app puts your details into that website's form on your phone. Nothing is submitted until you submit it yourself. What you submit goes to the settlement administrator that runs that website, under its own privacy policy, not ours.

Claim websites you visit inside the app may use cookies and similar technology, under their own policies.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone for deadlines and payment dates. They're created and delivered on your device; no reminder information is sent to us.

The letter checker

When you paste a letter or email into the letter checker, it's checked on your device. The text isn't sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or advertising tools in the app.
  • We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. Claimwell isn't directed to children.

Information others may collect

  • Apple and Google may collect information when you download or use apps, under their own policies. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may share crash reports with us. These don't include what you entered in Claimwell.
  • The company that hosts the Claimwell website may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested), as web servers do, under its own policy.

Erasing your information

  • App: My claims → Your data → Erase everything on this phone. Deleting the app also erases everything.
  • Website: My claims → Your data → Erase everything in this browser, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

Settlement information

The settlement details in Claimwell (deadlines, eligibility, payouts) come from public court-approved notices and official settlement websites. They're the same for everyone and contain no information about you.

Changes

If we change how Claimwell handles information, we'll update this policy and its effective date. If we ever start collecting personal information, we'll say so here before we do, and in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact.claimwell@gmail.com

Claimwell is an independent guide. It isn't a law firm and isn't affiliated with any court, settlement administrator or company named here, and nothing here is legal advice. A settlement isn't a court finding that the company did anything wrong. The court-appointed administrator decides every claim, and you file each claim yourself on the official settlement site.
