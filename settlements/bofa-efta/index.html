Who's included

  • People who told Bank of America that a debit card or ATM transaction on their account wasn't authorized.
  • Bank of America denied that claim between May 6, 2023 and July 14, 2026.
  • If you got a postcard or email notice about this settlement, the parties believe you may be in the class.
