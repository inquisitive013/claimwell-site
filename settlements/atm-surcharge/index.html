Who's included

  • Paid a surcharge to withdraw cash at an independent (not bank-owned) ATM in the U.S., like at a gas station, store, bar or hotel.
  • Used a debit or ATM card from a bank account (not a credit or prepaid card).
  • Any time from Oct 24, 2007 to Aug 14, 2026, and your bank didn't refund the fee.

The official notice has the exact definition. If you're not sure, A.B. Data, Ltd. can tell you before the deadline.
