Who's included

  • U.S. residents whose personal information was involved in the May 2025 cyberattack on Columbia University.
  • You were sent a notice about the cyberattack. Only people with a Notice ID can claim.
  • Columbia estimates about 2.97 million people are in the class.
  • The lawsuit says exposed data included names, Social Security numbers, financial aid, admissions and health insurance details.

How much you could get

Up to $8,500 with loss proof, or a cash share

Choose one: up to $8,500 for documented losses on or after May 16, 2025, or a cash payment from what's left of the fund after fees, costs and loss claims (amount not set). You can also sign up for two years of free credit and dark web monitoring, identity theft insurance up to $1,000,000 and identity recovery help. Payments may be reduced pro rata depending on how many people file. If your loss award would be smaller than the cash payment, you get the larger amount.

Total settlement fund: $16,125,000.

The final amount depends on how many people file. The administrator sets it after the deadline.

What you'll need

  • Your Notice ID from the postcard or email you got about the settlement.
  • Pick either a cash payment or reimbursement for documented losses (up to $8,500).
  • For losses: receipts or statements for costs on or after May 16, 2025.
  • Optionally sign up for 2 years of free credit monitoring.
  • File online, or mail a paper form postmarked by January 21, 2027.

Needs the ID from your notice. You need the unique Notice ID from the notice you were sent. The settlement is a closed class. Receipts or other documents are needed only if you claim reimbursement for documented losses. Those losses must have happened on or after May 16, 2025. Handwritten or self-made receipts alone are not enough.

How to file

  1. Go to the official settlement site, columbiauniversitydatasettlement.com. If an email or text sent you here, type the address yourself rather than tapping its link.
  2. Fill in the claim form. Have the items above ready.
  3. Choose how you want to be paid, if the form asks.
  4. Submit it by January 21, 2027 and keep the confirmation number.

Only file if you qualify. Claim forms are signed under penalty of perjury, and administrators check claims.

Filing keeps you in the settlement and gives up your right to sue the company separately over the same issue. The notice explains your other options, including excluding yourself.
