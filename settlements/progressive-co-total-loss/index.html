Who's included

  • Colorado residents with a Progressive Direct (from Apr 12, 2019) or Progressive Preferred (from Dec 19, 2021) auto policy.
  • Your car was declared a total loss on your own claim, through Jul 8, 2026.
  • The payout used a valuation report with a "projected sold adjustment".
