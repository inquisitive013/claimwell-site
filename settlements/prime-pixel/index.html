How much you could get

$20

Patient Portal Subclass Members may claim a one-time $20 cash payment by submitting a valid and timely Claim Form. Patient Portal and Email Subclass Members automatically receive a code for one year of CyEx Medical Shield Pro with their Notice.

What you'll need

  • You can claim $20 only if you used a Prime hospital's online patient portal during the dates listed for that hospital.
  • File online at phsettlement.com or mail the paper form, postmarked by December 28, 2026.
  • If you got a notice, it has a free one-year CyEx Medical Shield Pro code. Activate it by April 20, 2027. Enrollment asks for your Social Security number.
  • Cash is paid only after the court approves the deal (hearing March 9, 2027) and any appeals end.

Proof requirement not confirmed. The site does not say what the claim form asks for, or whether you need an ID from your notice. The free CyEx Medical Shield Pro code comes with the notice and needs no claim.

How to file

  1. Go to the official settlement site, phsettlement.com. If an email or text sent you here, type the address yourself rather than tapping its link.
  2. Fill in the claim form. Have the items above ready.
  3. Choose how you want to be paid, if the form asks.
  4. Submit it by December 28, 2026 and keep the confirmation number.

Only file if you qualify. Claim forms are signed under penalty of perjury, and administrators check claims.
