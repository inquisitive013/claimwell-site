Who's included

  • People in the U.S. or its territories who paid for a prescription at a Kroger pharmacy using their insurance.
  • Purchases from December 9, 2018 through August 23, 2026.
  • Covers pharmacies owned or run by Kroger or its affiliates. The lawsuit says Kroger's reported prices should have counted its Savings Club prices; Kroger denies this.
  • Not included: people who already sued, went to arbitration, or settled with Kroger over this same issue.

The official notice has the exact definition. If you're not sure, Angeion Group can tell you before the deadline.

How much you could get

Share based on amount paid; not set

Kroger pays $17,000,000. Attorneys' fees (up to one third), expenses, service awards and admin costs come out first. The rest is split pro rata among valid claims, based on how much each person paid for prescriptions at Kroger using insurance compared with everyone else's claims. The allocation plan may change until the Court approves it.

Total settlement fund: $17,000,000.

The final amount depends on how many people file. The administrator sets it after the deadline.

What you'll need

  • File a claim by December 21, 2026 at krogersavingsclubsettlement.com.
  • If you got a notice with a claim ID number, have it handy.
  • Estimate how much you paid for prescriptions at Kroger using insurance during the period.
  • If you claim $8,000 or more, or you're not on Kroger's customer list, you'll need records of your payments (for example, pharmacy receipts or history).

Proof optional; it can raise the payment. If you got a notice with a claim ID, use it. Claims of $8,000 or more always need records showing your Kroger prescription payments. If you file without being on Kroger's list of known customers, the administrator will contact you and ask for records of your purchases.

How to file

  1. Go to the official settlement site, krogersavingsclubsettlement.com. If an email or text sent you here, type the address yourself rather than tapping its link.
  2. Fill in the claim form. Have the items above ready.
  3. Choose how you want to be paid, if the form asks.
  4. Submit it by December 21, 2026 and keep the confirmation number.

Only file if you qualify. Claim forms are signed under penalty of perjury, and administrators check claims.

Filing keeps you in the settlement and gives up your right to sue the company separately over the same issue. The notice explains your other options, including excluding yourself.
