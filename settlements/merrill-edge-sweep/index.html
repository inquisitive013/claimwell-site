Who's included

  • Held a Merrill Edge self-directed retirement account (IRA, Roth, rollover, SEP or SIMPLE).
  • Cash in it was swept into Merrill-affiliated bank deposits between Dec 15, 2016 and Mar 15, 2020.

The official notice has the exact definition. If you're not sure, JND Legal Administration can tell you before the deadline.

Based on your balance; not set

Pro rata, based on the interest you'd have earned at a reasonable rate. Payments under $10 aren't issued.

Total settlement fund: $39 million.

The administrator works out each person's payment from the settlement's formula.

What you'll need

  • Nothing if you still have the account.
  • Former account holders: a current address, or an electronic payment request by Mar 26, 2027.

No proof needed. No claim needed. Paid into your Merrill Edge account, or by check if you closed it.

How you get paid

There's no claim form. People in the settlement are paid automatically. If you've moved, update your address with JND Legal Administration through the official site by March 26, 2027 so the payment reaches you.
